How I Work
My process on every project —
structured, repeatable, always research-first.

Before I open Figma, I understand the domain. Before I propose a direction, I've spoken to the people who live inside the problem. Every project below follows this agenda — from brief to validated outcome.

1
Project Brief & Context
The assignment, business case, technical constraints, and the design opportunity I'm being asked to unlock.
4
Ideation
Information architecture, interaction patterns, design principles — the structure before the pixels.
2
Discovery & Research
Contextual inquiry on-site, user interviews, CSAT data, competitive benchmarking — what people actually do, not what they say they do.
5
Design & Iteration
v1 to v4+ evolution, design system integration, before/after comparisons — decisions made visible.
3
Define
Persona synthesis, user journey mapping, key insights — turning raw research into a problem worth solving.
6
North Star Vision
Hi-fi at full prototype fidelity — the design I'd stake my name on, tested and ready for stakeholder alignment.
7
Decisions & Impact
Three decisions I would defend, phased delivery plan, and the business metrics that tell me the design worked.

Each case study below shows this agenda applied to a real enterprise brief. The research panels are windows into steps 2 & 3 — the thinking before the design.

ILM Policy Builder

Prisma Cloud Onboarding

Unified fragmented multi-cloud onboarding across AWS, Azure, and GCP into a single 3-step flow — 57% fewer screens, 8× customer sentiment improvement, 150% credit consumption growth within 6 weeks of launch.

Research across 4 personas — Cloud Security Engineers, IT Operations, Super Admins, and System Administrators — revealed per-cloud fragmentation as the core activation blocker. Oracle Unity Data Platform

Transformed the legacy MCPS-Manager JSON tool into a visual Admin Console — system administrators now create job configurations, ingest data, and manage schemas 70% faster with zero JSON required.

Admins previously had to manually parse and manipulate raw JSON files to configure data ingestion. A 6-phase redesign within Oracle's Redwood Design System replaced this with a fully visual interface — enabling self-service for System Administrators without requiring data architects to write code.

Oracle Live Experience

Designed a context-first customer engagement platform — progressive escalation, real-time annotations, and intelligent routing across iOS, Android, and Web. Reduced support costs while dramatically improving CSAT.

Customer support was fragmented across disconnected channels — associates lacked real-time visibility and customers had to repeat context across every handoff. The new platform combined SMS-to-Screenshare escalation with live annotations, cutting AHT by 60–90 seconds per call and reducing follow-up tickets by 15–20%.

About

Design direction,
not just design craft

I'm a full-stack Principal Designer with 15+ years embedded in Engineering, Product Management, and cross-functional teams across enterprise security, cloud, and data platforms. I translate complex systems into validated, scalable experiences — owning the full arc from user research and interaction design through to design systems and AI-integrated team practice.

I take on the hardest problems — where the domain is genuinely complex, users are experts under pressure, and getting the interaction wrong costs real money or real time. I've done on-site contextual inquiry with healthcare systems, government transportation agencies, and Fortune 500 enterprises across three continents.

I take AI in design practice seriously — not as a shortcut, but as a shift in how design teams think and work. I've facilitated Tree of Thoughts prompting workshops and Figma Make adoption programmes, moving teams from scepticism to fluency.
