Design Process

Research & Strategy

The Challenge

Prisma Cloud's onboarding for AWS, Azure, and GCP had grown organically into fragmented, inconsistent flows — 8 to 10 steps each with separate interfaces, different patterns for each cloud. Enterprise customers were dropping off during setup.

Research & Discovery

  • Contextual inquiry with enterprise teams
  • Tested with Informatica, Unacademy, Nvidia
  • Identified cloud-agnostic mental models

The Solution

Redesigned the entire experience as a unified, cloud-agnostic 3-step modal that uses progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load. Each step validates the user's configuration before moving forward.

Impact

  • 57% fewer screens compared to previous flows
  • 150% increase in credit consumption within 6 weeks
  • 8× improvement in customer sentiment scores
  • 120% growth in agentless feature adoption
